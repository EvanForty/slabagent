Questions

Which wallets can I connect?

Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and any wallet that supports the Solana Wallet Standard. On mobile, open the site inside your wallet's built-in browser.

Does SlabAgent hold my cards?

No. The agent trades with its own treasury. Physical slabs stay in Collector Crypt's insured vaults, represented by NFTs on Solana.

What happens when a trade loses money?

The loss comes out of the agent's trading capital and nothing is bought back for that trade. Only profitable trades trigger a buyback.

Is connecting my wallet safe?

Connecting only shares your public address. SlabAgent never asks for your seed phrase, and every transaction needs your approval in the wallet.

Is the trade data real?

Not yet. Until launch, the live agent page and ledger run a simulation built on the agent's rules. At launch they switch to the agent's on-chain transactions.
